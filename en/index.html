Personal concept · Spec

“Absence” — fragrance film

A bedroom, a wait, an understated bottle, an emptiness. No voice-over. No brand super. The fragrance is not a packshot: it is a trace. Storyboard + animatic stills — the standard stage before committing to a shoot.

~75″ · animatic stills 16:9 · B&W Fragrance & Beauty 10 shots · hard cuts Concept by César Cabrera

Animatic stills · FFmpeg hard cuts · 70″ of shots + 5″ end-credits fade · Download MP4

Insight: time does not pass. She fills the wait with private gestures. An unnamed bottle, hand-sized. Then the room is empty. Black. Refusing to “name” — the fragrance remains a presence, not a claim.
Act I · Room

ROOM · WAIT

Shots 01–03 · ~18″
  • 0:00Dressing table. MCU. She touches up her make-up. Eyes to camera. The frame watches her wait.
  • 0:07Bed. MS. Seated on the edge of the mattress, hands clasped. Stillness. Worn wall, rumpled sheets.
  • 0:13Hands. MLS. A private micro-gesture — crossed hands, cast shadow. Filling time.
Act II · Gestures

GESTURE · TRACE · SEEN

Shots 04–07 · ~30″
  • 0:18Wardrobe. MS. A coat held — the garment as delay. Still waiting.
  • 0:26Private spray. MCU. A single blank crystal bottle (hand-sized). A mist on the wrist. Not a packshot.
  • 0:34Gaze · hold. CU. Eyes into the lens. Zero product in frame. She knows she is being watched.
  • 0:40Window · night. MLS. Skyline, reflections. Observed, not performing.
Act III · Absence

TRACE · AFTER · HOLD

Shots 08–10 · ~22″ + end credits
  • 0:48Bottle insert. Anonymous crystal on wood. Light only. No type, no logo.
  • 0:56Empty room. WS. She is no longer there. Sheets, chair, residual light.
  • 1:04End credits · César Cabrera. Black background, centred typographic credit (Didot). No brand super. No phone / no hang-up.

Production notes: hand-sized bottle only · blank glass · 2 beats max · no logo · animatic stills = deliverable ahead of executive producer / quote / shoot. Possible 360° adaptations (16:9 hero · 9:16 stories) after creative lock.
